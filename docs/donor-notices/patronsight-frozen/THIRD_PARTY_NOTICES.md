Keep each applicable copyright, permission, disclaimer, licence and attribution
text with the source/binary distribution, including notices compiled into
native libraries. Copied donor/DINO code retains its original provenance and
dated adaptation notices. DINO, YuNet/SFace, Whisper and Silero model grants
remain distinct from code/native-runtime grants.

Ultralytics/TrackTrack and covered combinations use the accepted AGPL route.
FFmpeg/PyAV/OpenCV native constituents require their own GPL/LGPL source,
build and replacement obligations; wrapper BSD/Apache labels are insufficient.
MPL components require matching covered source availability. Windows MSVC and
DirectML platform binaries retain their separate Microsoft terms; no proprietary
restriction may be imposed on covered AGPL source.

Restricted InsightFace/MSMT17 assets are excluded from the proposed commercial
payload. The notice superset includes unused development candidates for audit
preservation, not permission to distribute those assets.

The final release must identify its supplier entity/release ID and reconcile
every shipped file to licence, notice, source and fulfilled conditions. Draft
notice files are usable inputs, but the payload-specific distribution collection
and source medium remain Opus work. No source offer has been issued.
